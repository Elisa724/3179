The Capital City Surge

Inflation has not fallen equally across Australia. While eastern seaboard capitals like Sydney and Melbourne experienced moderated headline figures near 3.2%, smaller and regional economies such as Adelaide (4.4%) and Hobart (4.5%) faced sustained supply bottlenecks and sharper housing overheads.
