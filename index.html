1. Geographic Baseline & The Non-Negotiable Squeeze

Tracking spatial inflation distribution alongside the divergence between essential and discretionary living expenses.

The Dual Pressure Points: Capital Disparities vs. The Inelastic Basket

Australia's inflation surge has been structurally uneven across state capitals. While headline annual rates moderated around 3.2% in Sydney and Melbourne, smaller economies like Adelaide (4.4%) and Hobart (4.5%) faced persistent supply frictions. Crucially, the burden is concentrated in non-negotiable living essentials—housing, food, and energy grew at more than double the rate of discretionary recreational spending.

Geographic Note: State-level inflation is proxied by the respective Greater Capital City Statistical Area (e.g., Sydney for NSW, Melbourne for VIC) per ABS Consumer Price Index methodology (ABS Cat 6401.0, Table 16). Non-discretionary basket encompasses essential household expenditures including food, housing, energy, health, and transport.
