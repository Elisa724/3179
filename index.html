The Dual Pressure Points

Australia's inflation surge has been structurally uneven. While headline numbers stabilized around 3.2%–4.5% across major state capitals, the underlying burden is concentrated in inelastic household essentials.


Non-negotiable living costs such as housing, health, and education accelerated significantly faster than discretionary goods like furnishings and recreational leisure.
